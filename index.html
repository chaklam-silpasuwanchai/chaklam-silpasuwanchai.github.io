From Hong Kong, I am an Assistant Professor in the Computer Science and Communication Technology program at the Asian Institute of Technology (AIT), Thailand.

My research group, AIT Brain Lab, focuses on Human-AI Interaction, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Brain Computer Interfaces.

I am an active author and reviewer of CHI, ACL, and NeurIPS. My research has received CHI Best Paper Awards in 2017 and 2026.

My research has been supported by several grants including the Gates Foundation, Toyota Mobility Foundation , Ramathibodi Hospital (spinal medical image analysis), the JSPS KAKENHI Young Scientists Grant, the Thailand Research Fund Young Scientist Grant, and the Toray Science Foundation Research Grant. I have also contributed as Co-PI to projects supported by JST and NBTC.

I completed my Ph.D. and postdoctoral research under the supervision of Professor Xiangshi Ren at the Center for Human-Engaged Computing.

Beyond academia, I am a co-founder of AI Brain Lab.
